== ENGAGEMENT GROUND RULES (Summary) ==
These come from the formal "Rules of Engagement" document given to every client:
1. Confidentiality — Client information is never released to third parties. Even under a court summons or IRS demand, we notify the client first and wait for permission before releasing anything.
2. Engagement letters — Every job has one. We only do what is written down and authorized.
3. Payment before work — No work begins until fees are agreed in writing and payment clears. For ongoing clients, the monthly fee must clear before that period's work goes into production.
4. Project lead time — Standard 2-week turnaround from when the client provides the last needed piece of information. If requested info is not provided within 2 weeks (10 business days), the engagement may be voided/archived. Client gets another 30 days to complete it; no refund for unfinished work in that case.
5. Change orders — Once a job is in production, any new request requires a separate change-order engagement and fee.
6. Free mobile app & portal access — Included for clients with a monthly engagement.
7. Free planning ideas — We give business owners money-saving ideas at no charge; implementing them requires a separate engagement and fee.
8. Decisions stay with the client — We advise; the client decides.
9. Information timing — Info provided less than 20 days before a deadline is treated as late; a rush fee (25% of the original engagement price, or $350, whichever is higher) may apply.
10. No legal or investment advice — Ever.
11. No audit, review, or compilation services — We do prepare financial statements for tax and limited purposes only.
12. Record retention — Work papers kept 3 years (scanned copies only). Clients are responsible for retaining their own source documents.
13. Auto-renewal — Engagements renew automatically unless otherwise communicated.
14. Communication policy — We expect a response within 3 days of a request. After 2 weeks of no contact via phone/email/text, it is treated as a communication breakdown, which can lead to archiving the job.
Client Bill of Rights: Right to respectful treatment, full value for money, complete satisfaction, quick service with honest delay notice, and prompt, knowledgeable answers.

== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ==

Q: What documents do I need for tax preparation?
A: Generally, W-2s, 1099s, prior year tax returns, receipts for deductions, and any investment statements. Our team will provide a full checklist during your consultation.

Q: How long does tax preparation take?
A: It depends on the complexity. Simple returns may take a few days. Complex business returns may take one to two weeks. Our workflow is designed to finish most jobs in two weeks, unless specified in engagement letter.

Q: Can you handle both personal and business taxes?
A: Yes. We handle personal, business, and multi-entity tax situations.

Q: Do you work with clients remotely?
A: Yes. We work with clients across the United States. Most services are available virtually via phone or video call.

Q: What if I received an IRS notice?
A: Do not ignore it. Contact us immediately. We provide full IRS audit representation and response services.

Q: Can I get help with back taxes?

Q: Do you help new businesses?
